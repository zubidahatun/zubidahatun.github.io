Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how IGVIO handles information when you visit and use the website.

Last updated: August 19, 2026

At IGVIO, we value privacy and aim to provide a simple and transparent browsing experience. This policy describes the general types of information that may be processed when you interact with the website and how that information may be used.

1. Information We Receive

IGVIO may receive information that you voluntarily enter into website features, such as an Instagram username submitted through a profile search form.

You should never enter passwords, authentication codes, financial information, or other sensitive account credentials into an Instagram viewer or any other third-party website.

2. How Information May Be Used

Information submitted through the website may be used to provide the requested functionality, display available search results, maintain website functionality, troubleshoot technical issues, and improve the overall user experience.

IGVIO does not require users to provide an Instagram password to use the profile viewer.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

IGVIO may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies for essential website functionality, preferences, security, analytics, or performance measurement.

Cookies may help the website understand general usage patterns and improve performance. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, although certain website features may not work as intended when cookies are disabled.

4. Analytics

IGVIO may use analytics services to understand general website traffic, performance, and visitor behavior. Analytics providers may collect information such as device type, browser information, approximate location, pages visited, and other technical data according to their own policies.

Analytics information is generally used in an aggregated or statistical manner to help improve the website.

5. Third-Party Services

IGVIO may rely on third-party services for hosting, analytics, security, performance monitoring, fonts, or other technical functionality.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. IGVIO does not control how an independent third-party service processes information.

6. Instagram Disclaimer

IGVIO is an independent website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected with Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc.

Instagram and related names, logos, and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

7. Private Account Information

Instagram account privacy settings are controlled by Instagram and the account owner. IGVIO does not encourage unauthorized access to restricted accounts or attempts to bypass privacy controls.

Users should respect the privacy settings and choices of other Instagram users.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security and reliability of the website. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

For your own security, never submit passwords, authentication codes, payment information, or other confidential credentials to IGVIO.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for website functionality, security, analytics, troubleshooting, or other legitimate operational purposes.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the service processing it.

10. Your Choices

You can choose not to submit information to optional website features. You can also manage cookies and certain tracking technologies through your browser settings.

If you have questions about information associated with your use of the website, you may contact the website owner through the contact page.

11. Children's Privacy

IGVIO is not intended to collect personal information from children. We encourage parents and guardians to supervise children's use of internet services.

12. External Links

The website may contain links to external websites or services. IGVIO is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external service before providing it with personal information.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services, technology, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

14. Contact IGVIO

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the website owner through the IGVIO Contact page.
