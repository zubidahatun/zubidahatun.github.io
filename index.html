FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is an Instagram viewer?

An Instagram viewer is a tool that helps users search for and explore available information associated with Instagram profiles.

Do I need an Instagram password to use IGVIO?

No. IGVIO does not ask you to enter an Instagram password. Use the username search field to begin.

Can IGVIO access private Instagram posts?

Private Instagram content is controlled by Instagram's privacy settings. Third-party websites should not claim to bypass those settings. IGVIO focuses on information and content that is available to its viewer.

Why can't some Instagram profiles be found?

A profile may be unavailable because the username is incorrect, the account has changed its username, the account is unavailable, or the requested information cannot be retrieved.

Does IGVIO work on mobile devices?

Yes. The website is designed with a responsive layout for phones, tablets, and desktop devices.
